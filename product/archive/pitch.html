DealerFinance

The buyer just enquires about a car. In the background, we have already worked out which lender will say yes.

Lender matching, sending and commission tracking for dealers, built on FinStack.

Problem statement

Who feels it: the Dealer Principal, the finance coordinator, the customer and the accounts team.

New-car dealers with tie-ups to several banks and NBFCs have no quick way to know which financier will approve a customer. So they guess, rejected cases bounce from lender to lender, customers wait or walk away, and the dealer's commission is chased on paper.

Why it is not solved today

There is no one place that holds the whole case. The pieces sit in three different systems, and cases and money leak in the gaps between them.

Scalable: the same engine, different lenders and rules

Some options and requirements will change from one line to the next. The underlying concept stays the same.

New car

What we built. Dealer price lists and road tax drive the loan amount.

Used car

Same flow. Our resale estimate (brand, engine size, year) sets the car's value, and the exchange value for a trade-in.

Two-wheeler

Smaller loans and higher volume. Faster rules, lighter checks.

Housing loan

Larger loans and property papers. More checks, lower commission rate.

Any commission business

Anywhere a seller refers a customer to a lender and is paid when the loan is disbursed.

What stays the same: enquiry and consent → credit check → match to lenders → send → follow the decision → commission. What changes: the lender rules, the price or valuation tables and the commission rates.

The flow

The full design has two menus: one for the sales team or the customer, and one for the finance coordinator or accounts. We did not have a database or a lending system in the hackathon, so we built a lean version: one linear journey that the customer or a dealer executive fills. What the hackathon build does: 6 steps and DigiLocker

1 Enquiry, OTP→DigiLocker→2 Verification→3 Price, tax, loan→4 Pick financier→5 Lender status→6 Settlement

Rejected at step 5 sends the executive back to step 4. Approved moves on to settlement: trade advance, balance and commission.

What we will do

Built in the hackathon

  • One linear journey, used by the customer or a dealer executive
  • Consent by OTP to the customer's phone, then DigiLocker for PAN, name and date of birth
  • Price, road tax, insurance, TCS, on-road price, exchange value and loan, worked out for the chosen car
  • Credit score and credit health from the bureau, and lender rules on top
  • Trade advance limit, balance and commission for four lenders: sbi, hdfc, bajaj and chola
  • Kia cars for the demo. More brands use the same lookup

Next, with database and lending-system access

  • Two menus: sales or customer, and finance coordinator or accounts
  • A real review queue, dashboards and a commission ledger with GST and TDS
  • Live trade advance balance and lender status from the lenders
  • Bank statement analysis and Account Aggregator
  • Used-car valuation, then two-wheeler and housing loans
