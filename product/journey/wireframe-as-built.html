DealerFinance: wireframe as built

The FinStack journey as it ran in the 13 Oct evening screen recording. One linear journey used by the customer or a dealer executive. Kia only. Lenders: sbi, hdfc, bajaj, chola. Name, phone, PAN, Aadhaar and vehicle number are masked. Header on every step: FinStack logo, "Customer_Flow", a language drop-down (English / Kannada) and a log-out icon. Each step ends with Previous and Continue buttons.

To check from the recording

Five things I noticed:
  1. Loan ignores the exchange value. Step 3 shows exchange value 3,13,000, but the loan is 15,17,728. That equals on-road - TCS - down payment (20,35,727 - 17,999 - 5,00,000) with no exchange taken off. With it taken off the loan would be 12,04,728. The loan may have been worked out before the exchange value filled in.
  2. Rejected gives an error. "Missing key 'approved_finance_amount'". The field only exists on the approved path. Give it a default of 0 so a rejection can go through.
  3. Income p.a is net of EMI. 25,20,000 = (2,50,000 - 40,000) x 12. A plain yearly income would be 30,00,000. Rename the label if that is intended.
  4. Credit Health is missing from Steps 4 and 5. It is still worked out, but the executive can't see it.
  5. Vehicle on Step 5 still shows the raw key, not the car's readable name.
