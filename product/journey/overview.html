DealerFinance

The buyer just enquires about a car. In the background, DealerFinance has already worked out which lender will say yes, so the dealer calls with an EMI, not a loan form.

Lender matching for new-car dealers, built on FinStack's Form Builder, Journey Builder and API Hub.

What it is

A tool the dealer uses to match each new-car buyer to the right bank or NBFC, send the case, and follow it until the money lands.

For

New-car dealers

Multi-brand dealers in Bengaluru who work with several banks and NBFCs. The dealer pays for it, like any software subscription.

Used by

The dealership team

The Dealer Principal runs it. The finance coordinator matches and sends. Sales staff enter leads. Each role sees only what it needs.

Customer

An individual car buyer

Mostly salaried, some self-employed. They fill one enquiry form, and the finance step happens quietly behind it.

The problem we are solving

Today the dealer decides which lender to try by habit and guesswork.

Problem statement New-car dealers with tie-ups to several banks and NBFCs have no quick way to know which financier will approve a customer. So they guess, rejected cases bounce from lender to lender, customers wait or walk away, and the dealer's commission is chased on paper.
Dealer Principal

Loses car sales when finance falls through. Has no view of which lender actually performs.

Finance coordinator

Guesses the lender, re-sends the same file, and chases status on phone calls.

Customer

Waits hours or days, and has several lenders check their credit one after another.

Accounts team

Tracks commission, GST and TDS on paper and finds short payments late.

How it works

Seven steps from enquiry to commission. The customer only sees the first and the third.

The same flow in words

Customer

Enquire about a car

The customer fills the car enquiry form, in the showroom or from a link. One extra line asks for consent to a soft credit check, with no effect on their score, and an OTP confirms it. No consent, no check: the lead waits.

Behind the scenes

Quiet credit check

With consent, a soft pull runs on name and mobile. If the bureau can't find the customer, PAN is asked and the check is retried. The score is used only to pick the lender. It is never passed on to the lenders.

Customer

Confirm identity

A DigiLocker OTP gives a verified name and address. PAN is verified at the start. The dealer never keeps copies of Aadhaar.

Behind the scenes

Match to lenders

Simple lender rules score each option as Likely, Possible or Not a fit. Rules cover credit score, loan against car price, EMI against income, income proof, enquiries in the last 3 months, missed payments and pin code. Where a lender needs proof, the customer uploads a bank statement and we read the income from it.

Dealer

Pick and send

The finance coordinator sees the ranked lenders with the EMI for each, and sends the case. Up to 3 lenders per case. A fourth needs the Dealer Principal's approval, and choosing below the top match needs a written reason.

Dealer

Follow the case

Each case moves through sent, under credit approval, approved, sanctioned and disbursed. A rejection sends it to the next lender instead of back to the start.

Dealer

Get paid

The commission ledger shows what each lender owes: commission plus 18% GST, less 2% TDS. It flags short payments. A trade advance tracker shows how much credit room is left with each lender.

How the order is decided

  1. Fit first: Likely before Possible. Not a fit is left out.
  2. The customer's preferred lender, if they have one.
  3. Lowest EMI after that.

The tags on each card (trade advance, commission, how fast the lender pays out) only break ties. They never push a customer to a costlier lender.

In the dealer portal, the lender is suggested by income proof and credit band: proof Yes and score above 750 goes to Bank 1, 700 to 750 to Bank 2, below 700 to NBFC 1.

Why it is different

Car makers already have finance portals. They work the other way round.

The subtle part: the customer never fills a loan form. Their credit consent sits inside the car enquiry they were going to submit anyway.

Safeguards

Consent first

No credit check, KYC or matching without recorded consent. Not even the Dealer Principal can override it.

Soft pull only

The credit report is used only to choose a lender. Each lender runs its own check on the case it receives.

Human review for edge cases

Name mismatches, no credit history, "Possible" fits, overrides and a fourth lender all go to one review queue with a reason on record.

Individuals only

Private new-car buyers. No businesses, no used cars, no top-ups.
